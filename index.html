01 · The flow

Three moves. One unbroken path.

No seams between the spark of an idea and the opening swap.

  1. 01

    Mint the idea

    Name it, give it a ticker and a face. One pass later it is a token that exists on Robinhood Chain.

  2. 02

    Choose its other half

    Set the asset, the community or the concept that lends the token its meaning. The counterpart is yours to pick, and the menu is wide.

  3. 03

    Trade with the curtain drawn

    Swaps take a quieter route, so your holdings stop being an announcement to everyone tracking the network.

    06 · Questions

    Straight answers.

    Can I buy anything today?

    Nothing in the records names a contract, so there is nothing to purchase. Any token claiming to be Initiate’s today did not originate here.

    Is anything live yet?

    The network figures are live. The launcher, the contract and the social accounts remain unannounced, and the records section spells out each one.

    Which network?

    Robinhood Chain. Its network number sits under the live figures above, taken from the record rather than typed here.

    Does private mean anonymous?

    Private describes intent, not a guarantee. Precise protections will be documented once the product ships, and until then nothing here is a promise.

    Where do the numbers come from?

    Block height and gas come from the chain’s own RPC. Value locked comes from DefiLlama. The moving tokens come from DexScreener. Any of them can lag or fail, and the page says so when they do.
